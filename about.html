About Me

Hello! My name is Ristin Sheikho, and I am an Informatics Engineer with a strong interest in web development and modern technologies. I am passionate about creating professional, attractive, and user-friendly websites that combine clean design with useful functionality. I enjoy turning ideas into real digital projects and continuously improving my skills through learning and practice.

My journey in web development has allowed me to build a solid foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP. I use these technologies to create structured, responsive, and interactive websites. I pay close attention to the visual appearance of every project while also focusing on usability and providing a smooth experience for users on different devices.

I believe that web development is a field that requires continuous learning. Technology is constantly evolving, and for this reason, I always try to develop myself by exploring new tools, improving my programming knowledge, and working on different projects. Every project gives me an opportunity to face new challenges, solve problems, and gain more practical experience.

One of my main goals is to become a highly skilled and professional software and web developer. I do not want to limit myself to the knowledge I already have. Instead, I am always looking forward to learning more advanced technologies, improving my problem-solving abilities, and gaining a deeper understanding of how modern web applications are designed and developed.

In the future, I hope to work on larger and more challenging projects where I can apply my knowledge, collaborate with other developers, and learn from real-world experiences. I believe that teamwork, creativity, and continuous learning are essential for becoming a successful developer.
